1. Connect your Instagram account

Click Connect Instagram — you'll approve access on Instagram's own page (OAuth). This app never sees your password. Once connected, press ⟳ Sync now any time to pull your followers and following lists automatically; every sync is saved as a snapshot, so the app can show who unfollowed you between syncs.

The Graph API only exposes follower/following lists for Business or Creator accounts, and the server needs a free Meta app configured (IG_APP_ID / IG_APP_SECRET in .env — see README). Personal accounts can skip connecting and use the manual upload below.

Server is not configured for Instagram login yet — set IG_APP_ID and IG_APP_SECRET in .env, restart, then connect.
